# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Eez Funnel a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior:

- Empathy and kindness.
- Respect for differing opinions.
- Constructive feedback.
- Accountability.
- Focus on community.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, insults, personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Other unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement

Report to conduct@eezfunnel.dev. All complaints reviewed and investigated.
