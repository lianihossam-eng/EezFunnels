Follow-up exact-head sweep fairness correction
- Sol's review of exact head `495eabd95ffffa17b980fd28655b28e292ac4897` found that a cursor-only R2 sweep could miss a late object inserted behind its saved cursor indefinitely if newer keys prevented the listing from reaching EOF.
- D22 now assigns each attempt a monotonic installation sequence in its unique R2 key and fixes a high-water sequence at the start of every sweep pass. The D1 restore rule requires preserving the sequence counter or registering a fresh R2 namespace.

This remains a deterministic Node model, not an R2/Cron integration test.
